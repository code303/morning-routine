# Origin and Exact Execution of the Viral "Tai Chi / Lymphatic" Morning Routine (9 exercises)

Research date: 2026-11-19. Most primary sources are TikTok captions (videos themselves could not be rendered/fetched, so only caption text seen via search snippets is cited) plus wellness blogs and lifestyle press. There is no authoritative instructional standard for this routine; the descriptions below are a consensus of creator and blog descriptions. Uncertainty is marked.

## Q1: Who popularized this exact set and when? How authentic is the "Tai Chi" label, and which traditions does each exercise map to?

### Takeaway
No single originator could be identified. The 9-move set (Lymphatic Hops, Body Waves, Trunk Twists, Arm Swings, Dead Arms, Golf Swings, Marches, Ballet Squats, Horse Stance) circulated on TikTok/Instagram in roughly late 2025 to 2026 as a user-shared format credited along a chain of fitness creators (e.g. @tulumtrainer, @fitwithhit, @mikerholland), and was reposted by accounts like @mydisciplinedrive and @wondwellness. The "Tai Chi" label is loose marketing: the moves resemble generic qigong/tai chi warm-ups ("shaking", arm swinging, toe bouncing, Ma Bu stance), but this is not a classical tai chi form. Some moves (golf swings, ballet/plié squats) are modern fitness additions.

### Cited Findings
**Social media spread / creators**

- An earlier @mydisciplinedrive post ("Day 5 of doing these viral Qi Gong movements every morning... my current 9-minute morning routine: Lymphatic Hops...") calls them "viral Qi Gong movements" (it already refers to the routine as viral, so it is not the originator). Per search-engine summary, that version's list was: Lymphatic Hops, Body Waves, Trunk Twists, Arm Swings, McGregors, Golf Swings, Ballet Squats, Marches, "Horseback Squats" (uncertain: summary, not verbatim). — [TikTok @mydisciplinedrive](https://www.tiktok.com/@mydisciplinedrive/video/7647796577427311894)
- @wondwellness (53-year-old creator) posted a variant: 1 Hops, 2 Body wave, 3 Arm swings, 4 Dead arms, 5 Golf swings, 6 March slap, 7 Trunk twist, 8 Windmill, 9 Plié squats; 1 min each; beginners 20 reps or 30 s each ("less than 5 min"); "simple mobility and lymphatic flow sequence inspired by movements used in Tai Chi and Qigong"; "Inspiration/credit: @tulumtrainer @fitwithhit @mikerholland". — [TikTok @wondwellness](https://www.tiktok.com/@wondwellness/video/7631388841072495903); [TikTok @wondwellness (2nd post)](https://www.tiktok.com/@wondwellness/video/7645299522293320990)

- mindbodygreen (published May 11, 2026) says the routine has been "making the rounds on social media" and "draws inspiration from QiGong and Tai Chi"; its 6-move version: body jumps, gentle jumps with hip turns, trunk twists, arms up and down, body waves, arm swings, 60 s each. Names no creator. — [mindbodygreen](https://www.mindbodygreen.com/articles/i-tried-viral-lymphatic-jump-routine-heres-what-surprised-me-most)

Shows the set existed by late 2025. — [BleuBird Substack](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- A fan-made "Move Timer" mixes the same moves (bouncing/shaking, arm swings, trunk twists, body waves, "Gold Swings" hip circles, "Dead Arms") and calls it "a blend of Chinese, Tibetan & modern practices". — [Move Timer](https://tmroyce.github.io/tmroyce-projects/random-projects/move-project.html)

**Mapping to traditional practices**
- Hops/bouncing ↔ qigong "shaking" warm-ups (e.g. Zang Fu Gong opens with shaking; 1–3 min, soft knees, bounce travelling through body) and the last of the Eight Brocades (Ba Duan Jin), "Bouncing on the Toes" (rise onto balls of feet, drop heels softly, inhale on rise, exhale on settle). — [Global Spiritual Studies](https://globalspiritualstudies.com/qigong-for-lymphatic-flow/)
- "Shaking the tree" qigong is cited as the source of the bouncing/shaking opener. — [Move Timer](https://tmroyce.github.io/tmroyce-projects/random-projects/move-project.html)
- Arm Swings / Dead Arms ↔ Shuai Shou Gong ("swinging hand exercise"): stand relaxed, feet hip-width, swing arms to shoulder height and let gravity drop them; variant adds a knee bounce every fifth swing; ~10 min/day to start; said to be >1,000 years old, part of Yang Sheng ("nourishing life"), commonly practised by retirees in Beijing's Temple of Heaven Park, recently popular on TikTok. — [Form Nutrition](https://formnutrition.com/?p=1157057)
- Horse Stance ↔ Ma Bu: "fundamental stance found in nearly all styles of wushu"; feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, parallel facing forward; knees ~90°, thighs ~parallel to ground; torso sinks straight down "as if hanging from a plumb line"; tailbone tucked; weight even; used for endurance; masters reportedly required holding ≥5 min (citation-needed claim). — [Wikipedia: Wushu stances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wushu_stances)
- Zhan Zhuang ("stance training"/"standing on stakes") is the category of stationary qigong holds; the Horse-Riding Stance is one of its most common forms. — [Shaolin Wahnam](https://shaolin.org/general-2/stance-training.html)
- Ma Bu hands: palms up on thighs, or fists at hip/waist/lowest rib. — [search summary of Shifu Yan Lei / related sources](https://www.shifuyanlei.co.uk/blogs/news/are-you-doing-it-right-horse-stance?page=2)
- Body wave is described as "a key Qigong movement for spinal health" by a fan site (low-authority claim). — [Move Timer](https://tmroyce.github.io/tmroyce-projects/random-projects/move-project.html)
- Contrast: a genuine tai chi/qigong morning routine looks different (Gathering the Chi, Opening and Closing, Raising and Pressing Down, Drawing the Bow [a Ba Duan Jin move], Floating Palms; slow, breath-led; 10–15 min). — [Yoga-Go / Welltech](https://yogago.welltech.com/content/morning-tai-chi-routine/)

**Evidence on "lymphatic" claims**
- No study shows qigong drains lymph or flushes toxins; no study of shaking and lymph flow; only qigong-lymphedema study is a 23-person breast cancer pilot. — [Global Spiritual Studies](https://globalspiritualstudies.com/qigong-for-lymphatic-flow/)
- Lymphatic therapist (via Healf): such moves support circulation but are "complements, not replacements". — [Healf](https://healf.com/blogs/health-journal/lymphatic-drainage-massage-explained)

### Inferences
- The 9-move "Tai Chi" set appears to be a condensation/remix of longer creator routines (Lanoue's 18-move routine is the most similar documented predecessor), relabelled with "Tai Chi/Qigong/Chinese" branding as it went viral. Calling it "Tai Chi" is inaccurate; "qigong-inspired warm-up/mobility flow" is fairer.
- Authenticity per exercise: High: Horse Stance (Ma Bu), Arm Swings/Dead Arms (Shuai Shou Gong / spinal-twist arm swinging common in Chinese parks), Hops (shaking/toe-bounce from Ba Duan Jin). Moderate: Trunk Twist (generic, but similar to loosening drills "turning the waist"). Low/modern: Body Wave (dance/fitness), Golf Swings (Western sports metaphor), Ballet/Plié Squats (ballet), Marching claps/slaps (aerobics-style; cross-body knee tap resembles qigong self-patting).

### Gaps
- Original creator and first date could not be confirmed; TikTok pages did not render, so profiles of @tulumtrainer, @fitwithhit, @mikerholland were not verified.
- No mainstream outlet (Healthline, Women's Health, Men's Health) coverage of this exact 9-move list was found.
- Claims that Shuai Shou Gong traces to Bodhidharma/Yi Jin Jing are commonly repeated online but were not found in a fetched source.

## Q2: How is each exercise performed step by step (key poses for stick-figure illustration)?

### Takeaway
All moves are standing, loose, rhythmic, momentum-driven; arms are mostly passive. Descriptions below merge creator/blog sources; breathing cues are rarely given (natural breath is the default), except in qigong-derived sources.

### Cited Findings
**1. Lymphatic Hops (opener)**
- Feet near floor, knees soft, arms relaxed at sides; small springy bounces in place, knees drop and rebound, no need for air; 30–60 s or 50–100 reps; beginners 10–30 s. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Feet hip-width; bounce on toes with heels slightly off ground; arms hang or swing gently; modification: calf raises. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Low-impact variant: keep toes down and lift heels quickly. — [Shore Local News](https://shorelocalnews.com/gentle-moves-lasting-benefits-a-simple-lymphatic-flow/)
- Key poses: (A) upright, soft knees, arms hanging; (B) feet just off floor / heels raised, arms loose.

**2. Body Wave**
- Start standing tall, arms overhead; swing arms down (behind) while dropping into a shallow squat; roll body from chest → upper back → mid back → lower back; reverse to stand; head steady facing forward; slight bounce in low back/knees. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Alternative: start in squat with hands near floor, roll up the spine vertebra by vertebra, sweeping arms overhead, reach tall, reverse continuously. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Clinic walkthrough: arms reach overhead then sweep behind as you squat. — [Insight Timer (summary)](https://insighttimer.com/kcgonz/guided-meditations/quick-lymphatic-drainage-sequence-for-inflammation)
- Key poses: (A) tall, arms straight overhead, slight back arch; (B) mid-wave: knees bent, chest forward, arms sweeping down; (C) bottom: shallow squat, arms swept behind hips, spine rounded.

**3. Arm Swings**
- One arm at a time swinging forward and back at shoulder height, shoulders relaxed; shake out if tired. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Alternating: one arm swings overhead while the other swings down/back, continuous rhythm, slight torso rotation; feet hip-width. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Shuai Shou variant: both arms swing forward to shoulder height, gravity drops them back; knee bounce every 5th swing. — [Form Nutrition](https://formnutrition.com/?p=1157057)
- Key poses: (A) one arm forward-up, other back-down ("pendulum scissor"); (B) mirrored.

**4. Dead Arms**
- Arms fully limp; rotate torso side to side so arms swing across and slap gently against sides/ribs/back; feet hip-width; no arm muscle engagement. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Raise arms and let them go limp; sway body so arms slap torso; "twist the waist" variant: full left/right twist with dead arms. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Variant: tap each shoulder, then alternate arms swinging loosely back and forth. — [Shore Local News](https://shorelocalnews.com/gentle-moves-lasting-benefits-a-simple-lymphatic-flow/)
- Key poses: torso rotated left, one arm wrapped across front (hand hitting opposite hip/side), other arm wrapped behind back (hand hitting lower back); mirrored.

**5. Trunk Twist**
- Feet hip-width, slight forward lean, back not arched; twist torso side to side, arms swing across body; feet and hips face forward. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Feet shoulder-width, knees soft, arms hang loose following momentum, hips relatively stable. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Variants: hands on hips twisting ([Move Timer](https://tmroyce.github.io/tmroyce-projects/random-projects/move-project.html)); bent slightly at hips, hands on shoulders, elbows out, twist ("upper back twists") ([Shore Local News](https://shorelocalnews.com/gentle-moves-lasting-benefits-a-simple-lymphatic-flow/)).
- Key poses: hips square forward, shoulders rotated ~45–90°; arms loosely across/behind.

**6. Marching Claps / March Slaps**
- March in place lifting one knee at a time; swing opposite arm and slap the rising knee; fling arms back to stretch upper back; lean slightly forward. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Lift knees toward hip height, tap with one or both hands, upright, core engaged; smaller marches fine. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Opposite-hand knee tap crosses the body's midline. — [search summary, ageosophy Substack](https://ageosophy.substack.com/p/morning-energy-stack)
- Key poses: standing on one leg, other knee up near hip height, opposite hand touching that knee, free arm flung back.

**7. Golf Swings**
- Feet shoulder-width; arms together swing side to side like a pendulum/golf club; don't fling arms out; let whole body rotate incl. legs (pivot back foot) to protect knees. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Rotate side to side as if taking golf swings, loose arms lifting fairly high, weight shifts foot to foot. — [BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)
- Key poses: hands clasped/together; (A) arms high over right shoulder, torso turned right, left heel lifted; (B) through bottom (arms down in front); (C) mirrored high left.
- Note: one fan site renames this "Gold Swings" = figure-8 hip circles; likely a garbling. — [Move Timer](https://tmroyce.github.io/tmroyce-projects/random-projects/move-project.html)

**8. Ballet Squats (Plié squats)**
- Wide stance (wider than shoulders), toes turned out slightly; drop while swinging arms down, push up while swinging arms up; back straight, avoid bending over. — [Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)
- Squat wide, then lift arms wide as you stand. — [Shore Local News](https://shorelocalnews.com/gentle-moves-lasting-benefits-a-simple-lymphatic-flow/)
- Key poses: (A) wide turned-out stance, knees bent over toes, upright torso, arms down/crossed in front; (B) standing tall, arms raised wide in V overhead.

**9. Horse Stance (finisher/static hold)**
- Ma Bu: feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, parallel facing forward; knees bent (traditional ~90°, thighs ~parallel); torso vertical, tailbone tucked; weight even. — [Wikipedia: Wushu stances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wushu_stances)
- Hands palms up on thighs, or fists at hips/waist. — [Shifu Yan Lei](https://www.shifuyanlei.co.uk/blogs/news/are-you-doing-it-right-horse-stance?page=2)
- Variant "Horseback Squats" (dynamic version) in some posts. — [TikTok @mydisciplinedrive (summary)](https://www.tiktok.com/@mydisciplinedrive/video/7647796577427311894)
- Key pose: wide stance, deep knee bend, vertical torso, fists at hips or arms held forward at chest height (hugging-tree arms, a Zhan Zhuang convention - inference).

### Inferences
- Breathing: viral posts give no breathing cues; for illustration/app cues, natural rhythmic breathing during dynamic moves and slow belly breathing during horse stance are consistent with qigong sources (inhale on rise, exhale on settle for bouncing per Global Spiritual Studies).
- Body target regions (inferred from mechanics): Hops = calves, ankles, whole-body loosening; Body Wave = spine flexion/extension, hips, shoulders; Arm Swings = shoulders, chest/upper back; Dead Arms & Trunk Twist & Golf Swings = thoracic spine rotation, obliques, hips; Marching = hip flexors, core, coordination; Ballet Squats = adductors, glutes, quads; Horse Stance = quads, glutes, isometric endurance.

### Gaps
- No source specifically describing "Marching Claps" (clapping rather than slapping) was found; likely a variant where hands clap under the raised knee or in front of the chest — unverified.
- No video-frame-level verification of how the viral creators execute each move (TikTok not fetchable).

## Q3: Typical timing, order, and total length?

### Takeaway
Standard format: 9 moves × 60 s = ~9 min, done back-to-back, often with video on loop; beginner format 30 s each (~4.5–5 min) or 20 reps each. Order varies but typically: hops first, body wave second, rotational/arm moves in the middle, marching and squats late, horse stance last.

### Cited Findings
- 60 s each, order Hops → Body Waves → Trunk Twists → Arm Swings → Dead Arms → Golf Swings → Marches → Ballet Squats → Horse Stance (9 min). — [TikTok @mydisciplinedrive](https://www.tiktok.com/@mydisciplinedrive/video/7673973634586742048)
- 1 min each, Hops → Body wave → Arm swings → Dead arms → Golf swings → March slap → Trunk twist → Windmill → Plié squats; beginners 30 s or 20 reps (<5 min). — [TikTok @wondwellness](https://www.tiktok.com/@wondwellness/video/7645299522293320990)
- Another version uses 30 s per move with order Lymphatic Hops, Body Waves, Arm Swings, Dead Arms, Trunk Twists, Golf Swings, Marching, Ballet Squats, Horse Stance (per search summary of TikTok discover pages — uncertain). — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/tai-chi-exercise-workout)
- Related routines: 6 × 60 s ([mindbodygreen](https://www.mindbodygreen.com/articles/i-tried-viral-lymphatic-jump-routine-heres-what-surprised-me-most)); 8 × 30–60 s = 4–8 min ([BleuBird](https://bleubird1981.substack.com/p/the-chinese-longevity-routine-8-minutes)); 15 × 60–75 s ≈ 15 min ([Shore Local News](https://shorelocalnews.com/gentle-moves-lasting-benefits-a-simple-lymphatic-flow/)); 18 moves ≈ 20 min with progression 10–30 s (wk 1–2) → 30–60 s (wk 3–4) → 60 s/50–100 reps (wk 5+) ([Wildcard Wellness](https://wildcardwellness.co/blog/lymphatic-drainage-exercises)).

### Inferences
- A reasonable canonical sequence for an app: Hops → Body Wave → Arm Swings → Dead Arms → Trunk Twist → Marching (Claps) → Golf Swings → Ballet Squats → Horse Stance, 60 s each (9 min), with a 30 s "short" mode (4.5 min); this matches the user's listed order and the general gentle-to-strength progression, ending on the static Ma Bu hold.

### Gaps
- No evidence of rest intervals; posts imply continuous transitions.
